Personal Information

Give the reader a direct way to identify you and contact you. Keep it compact and professional.

DO

Use a professional email, location and relevant portfolio links.

AVOID

Adding unnecessary personal details such as age, marital status or a full home address.

RED FLAGS

Common CV mistakes.

NEEDS ATTENTION

A generic summary says almost nothing.

Phrases like “hard-working team player” are easy to write but give little evidence of what you can actually do.

BETTER APPROACH

Name your target role, strongest skills and the kind of problems you can solve.

TRY THIS
“Frontend developer focused on responsive interfaces, JavaScript and accessible user experiences.”
MAKE IT STRONGER

Before / After.

BEFORE

“I am a hardworking person who is interested in technology and wants to grow professionally.”

Too broad · little evidence · no target
AFTER

“Frontend developer building responsive, accessible interfaces with JavaScript and modern CSS, with a focus on practical user experience.”

Target role · skills · focus
A stronger sentence answers: What do you do? What are you good at? Where do you create value?
